Before we hitch up

We walk around every trailer first. It needs:

  • Current registration
  • Working lights and electric brakes
  • Tires and wheel bearings in road-ready shape
  • A 2 in or 2-5/16 in coupler
  • Loose items stowed and doors latched

Questions

Do I need to be there?

Not always. If the campground or storage lot allows drop-offs, we can deliver before you arrive and send photos once it's set.

Can you hook up power and water?

On request we connect shore power and water where the site has them. Sewer connections stay with you.

What if my trailer isn't road-ready?

If tires, bearings, lights or brakes don't pass the walk-around, we won't tow it until they're fixed. That keeps you, your trailer and everyone on the road safe.

How far will you go?

Most trips run from the Inland Empire to the mountains, desert and coast. Longer runs are quoted case by case.
